# Community standards

Effect Doctor welcomes precise disagreement about rules, evidence, and implementation. Keep discussion useful to the people doing the work.

- Critique code and claims, not people.
- State assumptions and provide reproducible evidence.
- Respect privacy, embargoes, and proprietary source.
- Do not harass, threaten, demean, or discriminate.
- Stop an interaction when a participant or maintainer asks you to disengage.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive. Report conduct concerns privately through [Ocarina Labs' get-in-touch page](https://www.ocarinalabs.ai/). Do not include sensitive evidence until a private channel is confirmed.
